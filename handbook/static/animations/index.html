Circadian Rhythm Visualisations

Interactive animations · Handbook supplement

Eight interactive animations covering the core concepts of circadian biology — from the mathematics of rhythmic signals to the molecular feedback loops that drive the ~24-hour clock. Each animation can be played, paused, reset, and scrubbed.

Sine Curve Components
Step-by-step reveal of a sinusoidal rhythm showing mesor (midline), amplitude (half the peak–trough range), and period (~24 h for circadian rhythms).

Periodogram
See how a χ²/Lomb–Scargle periodogram sweeps candidate sine waves across a range of periods and accumulates goodness-of-fit (R²) scores to identify the dominant period.

Folding & the Periodogram Statistic
Watch a record fold at a trial period and its bins average across cycles, then see the spread of the bin means become both the Enright amplitude and the Sokolove–Bushell χ² — the same numerator, swept to draw the periodogram.

Correlogram (ACF)
The autocorrelation function slides a copy of the signal across itself at increasing lags. Recurring peaks at 24 h and 48 h confirm the presence of a circadian rhythm.
