A periodogram (χ²/Lomb–Scargle style) identifies the dominant period in a time series. A test sine wave (top-left) is swept systematically from short to long periods. At each candidate period the wave is overlaid on the original data (top-right) and the goodness-of-fit (R²) is recorded. The bottom panel accumulates these values into the periodogram; the tallest bar reveals the dominant period — here near 23.5 h, consistent with a circadian rhythm.
