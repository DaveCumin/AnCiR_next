A circadian rhythm is commonly modelled as a sinusoidal function: y(t) = M + A · sin(2πt/τ + φ). Three parameters fully describe its shape: the mesor (M) — the mean level around which the rhythm oscillates; the amplitude (A) — half the distance from peak to trough; and the period (τ) — the time for one complete cycle (~24 h for circadian rhythms). The animation builds the curve step-by-step, then labels each component.
