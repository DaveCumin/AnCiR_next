The Fast Fourier Transform (FFT) decomposes any complex time series into a sum of sinusoidal components, each characterised by a frequency, amplitude, and phase. The animation uses a Discrete Fourier Transform (DFT) to show this process step by step. The composite signal (top) is built from a dominant 24 h component and a 12 h harmonic. As the DFT sweeps through candidate frequencies, the accumulating power spectrum (right) reveals peaks at exactly those periods — demonstrating how spectral analysis identifies circadian and ultradian components.
