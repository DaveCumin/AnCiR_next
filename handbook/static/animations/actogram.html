An actogram is the standard tool in chronobiology for visualising rhythmic activity over time. Raw activity data (left) is recorded continuously over multiple days. Each day is extracted and double-plotted side-by-side — so each day appears in the second half of one row and the first half of the next — then stacked vertically. This format reveals whether the rest–activity rhythm drifts, advances, or remains locked to a 24-hour cycle.

Gold bars = high activity; blue bars = rest/low activity.
