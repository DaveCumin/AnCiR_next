← All animations Molecular Clock Mechanism
The mammalian circadian clock is driven by a transcription-translation feedback loop (TTFL) that takes approximately 24 hours to complete. CLOCK and BMAL1 proteins heterodimerize in the nucleus and activate transcription of the Per and Cry genes via E-box elements. Once PER and CRY proteins accumulate in the cytoplasm, they form a repressive complex that translocates back to the nucleus and inhibits CLOCK:BMAL1 activity — thereby completing the ~24 h feedback loop.
