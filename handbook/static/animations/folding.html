← All animations Folding & the periodogram statistic
Both the Enright amplitude and the Sokolove–Bushell χ² periodograms are built from the same two moves. First the record is folded at a trial period — every cycle is laid on top of the last, forming a Buys–Ballot table (rows = cycles, columns = phase bins). Then each column is averaged across cycles to give the mean waveform (the bin means, or "form estimate"). The two methods differ only in how they summarise the spread of those bin means about the grand mean: Enright takes their root-mean-square, Aₚ = √(⟨(Ȳₕ − Ȳ)²⟩); Sokolove–Bushell scales the same sum of squares into a χ² with P − 1 degrees of freedom.
