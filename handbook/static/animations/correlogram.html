← All animations Correlogram (ACF)
The autocorrelation function (ACF) quantifies how similar a time series is to a lagged version of itself. At a lag equal to the circadian period (~24 h), the signal lines up with itself, producing a peak in the correlogram. Successive harmonics appear at 48 h, 72 h, etc. Dashed confidence-interval lines (±1.96/√n) separate statisticallysignificant correlations from noise.
